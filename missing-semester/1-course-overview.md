## The Shell 
### 구조 
- To open a shell prompt(where you can type commands), first need  terminal, which is the visual interface to a shell
- **On Linux and macOS**
	- this will usually open the Bourne Again SHell, or "bash" for short 
- **On Windows**
	- "batch" or "powershell" shells
### 왜 사용? 
- The shell is not just much faster than "clicking around", it also comes with expressive power you can't easily find in any one graphical program.
